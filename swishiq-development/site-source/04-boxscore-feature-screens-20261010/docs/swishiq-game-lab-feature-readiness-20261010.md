# Game Lab feature-family readiness

**Date:** 2026-10-10

**Scope:** Candidate100 and the pinned V4 research feature map. The source-readiness review is supplemented by completed development-only screens for rotation continuity and history reliability; it does not establish independent or prospective predictive validity. Candidate100 remains `development-unvalidated`.

## Current contract

Candidate100 activates 40 total-head and 39 margin-head predictors. Relevant active inputs already cover rolling score form, shrunk 20-game scoring, opponent-adjusted team strength, partial box-score shooting/assist rates, schedule pressure, home/away-role venue deviations, active-player count, and minute-share concentration. It does not activate explicit history-support/variance fields, rotation-continuity fields, complete Four Factors, pace, or efficiency ratings.

The pinned Candidate57 union cache keeps game identity/date, `observedThrough`, target labels, and feature maps separate. Its source manifest pins V4 `game-lab-inputs.json`, `team-games.json`, and `player-games.json`, plus the history, rotation, box-score, and venue builders. The source rows are flattened; player/team-game records are not embedded as raw rows in Candidate100's base feature map. New research inputs should keep semantic names in an isolated feature artifact and use only Candidate100 slots explicitly listed in `excludedFeatures`, following the existing Candidate100 alias-adapter pattern. Do not alter the Candidate100 configuration as part of this readiness review.

## Family details

### History reliability

- **Source and strict-prior rule:** Candidate10 consumes completed team scores (`gameRef`, season, local game date, home/away team, home/away score) and scoreless target schedule identity. It snapshots all targets on a local date before applying that date's outcomes; target score-like fields are not copied. The V4 wrapper requires loader-verified, complete `team-games` and `schedule-reconciliation` parts from the same exact-season package.
- **Available fields and formula:** The context emits `historyGameCount`, `priorSeasonGameCount`, the last 20 `priorGameRefs`, `pointsFor/Against20Shrunk`, and `pointsForSd20Shrunk`, `pointsAgainstSd20Shrunk`, and `marginSd20Shrunk`. A compact new reliability signal can be `r20 = n20 / (n20 + 5)`, where `n20 = min(current-season prior historyGameCount, 20)`. The denominator matches the existing 5-game prior weight on the 20-game score means. The existing volatility formula is `sqrt((sum((x - mean(x))^2) + 10 * priorVariance) / (max(0, n - 1) + 10))` over the latest 20 prior observations.
- **Shrinkage and missing behavior:** Current 5/20-game score means shrink toward the prior-season team mean (or league anchor) with prior weight 5; season means use weight 10. The SD estimates use 10 prior-variance pseudo-observations. A true `n20=0` can be represented as `r20=0` only when the verified context explicitly reports zero history; missing/unverified context is an error, not zero. The V4 wrapper currently requires at least one prior game for each side, so a candidate screen must use only targets that pass that contract.
- **Screen result:** Four strictly prior, source-pinned support additions were compared against rebuilt Candidate100 means with exact 6,824-row score-mean parity and then canonical uncertainty scoring on 4,920 2022-25 targets. `meanPriorSeasonReliability82` in the total head had a negligible favorable raw-total-MAE point difference of -0.00027, but its 95% paired interval was [-0.00694, 0.00808] and total squared error worsened. `currentSeasonReliabilityAdvantage20` in the margin head had small favorable Brier/log-loss point estimates, but clear home-score CRPS and interval-score harm. The other two additions had no primary point gain. No history feature is retained and no combination is justified. Evidence: [`summary.json`](../prototypes/swishiq-package-v4/experiment-tools/runs/history-reliability-individual/run-1791613941936-18308b31-287e-43af-95dc-05de988a822b/history-pipeline/run-1791614823239-26168/summary.json).

### Rotation change

- **Source fields:** The pinned `player-games` rows provide `entities.gameRef`, `teamCode`, and `playerRef`, with `values.minutes`, `values.isStarter`, regular-season `time`, and available evidence. The union-cache builder pairs these rows to reconciled team games and passes the rotation as player references, minutes, and starter flags.
- **Formula and window:** Candidate16 computes active-player overlap and starter overlap as Jaccard similarity between current and previous prior-game sets. Minute-share overlap is `sum(min(currentShare[player], previousShare[player]))`. It averages the latest five prior team games and shrinks each mean toward the prior-team or league mean with five prior-equivalent games. The whole target date is captured before any rotation from that date is applied. First-observation continuity is null and carries an explicit continuity sample count before the shrinkage fallback is used.
- **Current use and gap:** Candidate100 already selects `meanActivePlayerCount5` and `meanMinuteShareHhi5` (and their margin contrasts), but excludes the six active-player/starter/minute-share continuity slots. Those are the exact incremental rotation fields; do not re-propose shape metrics already selected.
- **Missing behavior / skip:** The builder rejects duplicate player references, invalid minute totals, 5–20 active players outside 200–400 total minutes, and any rotation without exactly five identified starters. Skip the affected screen rows or the family if paired-source coverage fails; do not turn absent player identities or unknown starters into an overlap score of zero.

### Opponent-adjusted Four Factors, pace, and efficiency

- **Source fields:** Candidate21 requires regular-season available `player-games` with `entities.gameRef/teamCode/playerRef` and `values.box` fields `points`, `fieldGoalAttempts`, `fieldGoalsMade`, `threePointersMade`, `freeThrowAttempts`, `offensiveRebounds`, `defensiveRebounds`, and `turnovers`. It pairs these to matched, training-eligible `team-games` with game/team/opponent identity, date/season, home flag, and points for/against; player point totals and paired team scores/dates must reconcile.
- **Formula and window:** Per game, eFG% is `(FGM + 0.5*3PM)/FGA`; turnover rate is `TOV/(FGA + 0.44*FTA - OREB + TOV)`; offensive-rebound rate is `OREB/(OREB + opponent DREB)`; free-throw rate is `FTA/FGA`. Estimated possessions use `FGA + 0.44*FTA - OREB + TOV`; pace is the mean of both sides' possessions; ORtg and defensive-allowance rating are points per estimated possession times 100. Candidate21 rolls the last 20 prior team games and shrinks each metric toward the prior-date league mean with prior weight 8. Its adjusted Four Factor rate is `ownRate - (opponentPriorAllowance - leagueAllowance)` using the opponent's prior-date 20-game allowance.
- **Current use and gap:** Candidate100 already has partial three-point attempt/pct, two-point pct, and assist-per-made-field-goal features, but no complete Four Factors, pace, or ORtg/DRtg features. Candidate21's pace and ratings are currently raw rolling values; it does not provide opponent-adjusted pace or efficiency. Define those adjustments before any screen rather than treating the unadjusted values as opponent-adjusted.
- **Missing behavior / skip:** Candidate21 rejects missing required box fields, zero/invalid denominators, absent player aggregates, non-reconciling points/date/season, or non-paired team sides. It does not silently impute. The exact fields are pinned in the V4 package, but they are not part of the flattened Candidate100 active feature map.

### Schedule pressure

- **Source fields and formula:** Candidate100 already selects mean rest days and games in the last seven days, margin rest and seven-day density advantages, and back-to-back / three-in-four / four-in-six / five-in-seven pressure. The V4 native feature code derives pressure from prior game dates and pregame `backToBack`: back-to-back is the scheduled-side flag; three-in-four is at least two prior games in four days; four-in-six is at least three in six days; five-in-seven is at least four in seven days. Total inputs average the two sides; margin contrasts are half the away-minus-home pressure. Candidate10's history context provides prior-date games-last-four/six counts, while the scheduled target input provides the target back-to-back flag and seven-day count.
- **Shrinkage and missing behavior:** These are binary pressure flags and schedule counts, not rolling-shrunk rates. The v5 transform maps a missing back-to-back flag to 0; history counts must come from the prior snapshot. The target date's results are never included.
- **Gap / skip:** The named pressure family is already represented in Candidate100. Skip another feature proposal over the same windows; reconsider only for a pre-specified new schedule construct that can be computed from target-known schedule information and completed past games.

### Venue drift

- **Source fields and formula:** Candidate100 already selects 10- and 20-game venue-role offense/defense deviations and margin advantages. Candidate57/Candidate24 distinguish `isHome` from away and compare recent same-role form with the team's all-role form. Candidate24 uses the last 10/20 prior games, shrinks the all-role mean toward the league scoring anchor (110 points) and the same-role mean toward the all-role mean with prior weight 8. Candidate24 targets require a non-neutral regular-season home/away schedule identity.
- **Missing behavior and gap:** No arena ID, venue coordinates, relocation date, or neutral-site venue map is in this feature contract. Candidate24 fails if a complete strictly prior venue context cannot be formed. Current role-based metrics cannot identify a new building or a team playing away from its nominal home arena.
- **Skip condition:** Skip a new arena-drift candidate until every target and prior game has a verified actual venue ID (including neutral-site handling), with dated mappings. Do not substitute home/away role for physical venue.

### Travel load

- **Existing contract:** Candidate10 v5 can consume an optional `swishiq-candidate25-travel-context-v1` with `sourceVenueMapVersion`, strict `observedThrough`, side-level `lastLegDistanceMiles`, `travelMilesLast7Days`, eastward/westward time-zone shift, `coldStart`, and `priorGameCount`. If supplied, it builds log-distance features with `log1p`, total/margin contrasts, time-zone shifts, and explicit cold-start/support features.
- **Formula/source/window:** The intended measures are distance from the previous actual game venue to the target actual venue, cumulative distance on prior itinerary legs in the previous seven days, and signed east/west time-zone displacement. Source data must include the target's actual venue and the actual venue for every relevant prior game, each joined to a versioned venue map with coordinates and timezone. This is a seven-day route window plus a last-leg measure; no shrinkage is specified in the optional feature transform.
- **Missing behavior and gap:** The consumer requires an explicit cold-start boolean/status and `priorGameCount`; its cold-start contract uses zero distances/shifts only with that explicit marker. But the reviewed Candidate57 source manifest does not pin a venue map, and no Candidate25 travel-context builder or travel feature family is present in the V4 models/experiment tools. The schedule fields alone do not reveal physical route or timezone.
- **Skip condition:** Skip until the actual-venue crosswalk and all target/prior venue coordinates/timezones are complete and version-pinned. Unknown venues must remain unknown or cause row exclusion, not be represented as zero travel.

## Recommendation

**Completed raw-rate screen:** Seven isolated Candidate21 pace/Four-Factor additions were tested in the total or margin head. None was retained. Offensive-rebound context in the total head was the only configuration with lower pooled MAE and RMSE, but its paired MAE interval crossed zero, its large positive signed-error shift was clear, and its season pattern was mixed. The full result table is in [`swishiq-candidate100-candidate21-raw-boxscore-screen-results-20261010.md`](swishiq-candidate100-candidate21-raw-boxscore-screen-results-20261010.md). A separate covariance audit found no tested C21 addition highly correlated (|Pearson r| >= 0.80) with active Candidate100 features on the development cohort.

**Completed adjusted-rate screen:** Eight single-predictor, single-head opponent-adjusted Four-Factor additions completed on the same cohort. No feature was retained. Adjusted eFG has tiny intended-MAE point gains but worse RMSE and distribution/probability evidence; all other additions worsen intended MAE. See [`swishiq-candidate100-adjusted-fourfactor-screen-results-20261010.md`](swishiq-candidate100-adjusted-fourfactor-screen-results-20261010.md).

**Remaining family disposition:** Candidate21 raw pace and ratings are not opponent-adjusted. A distinct opponent-adjusted pace/efficiency formulation must declare its correction, source history and fallback before screening. Named schedule density and venue-role features already exist, while travel and actual-venue drift lack a pinned dated venue crosswalk. Preserve those distinctions in the queue. Cycle 4 can proceed while these remaining feature dispositions are completed; no combination is justified by the completed screens.
